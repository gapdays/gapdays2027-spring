## Travel

The closest international airport is Edinburgh airport. From there, there are
various shuttle service or public transport options to St Andrews.

If travelling by train, the closest train station is Leuchars. You can get to
St Andrews in 15 minutes via the frequent 99 bus service.

Alternatives and further information on getting to St Andrews can be found on
[the university website](https://www.st-andrews.ac.uk/visiting/travel/).

## Accommodation

One option is the St Andrews Premier Inn Hotel, in the southern part of
St Andrews. There is also a range of B&Bs.

More information can be found [here](https://www.st-andrews.ac.uk/visiting/accommodation/).
